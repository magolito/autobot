Common questions.

Is my capital safe? What if the bot makes a bad trade?+
AutoBot runs a rules-based strategy built and managed by our team — it doesn't improvise. You choose your risk level, and stop-losses, position size limits and drawdown limits are enforced automatically on every trade. The bot cannot access or withdraw your funds — it only places orders. Your capital stays on your exchange at all times.
What is the minimum capital required?+
There's no hard minimum set by AutoBot. In practice, most clients start with $5,000–$10,000 to allow meaningful position sizing. Our team will help you understand what makes sense for your specific strategy on the onboarding call.
Can I pause or stop it at any time?+
Yes. You can pause, adjust, or stop at any time from your dashboard. You can also revoke API keys directly from your exchange — which instantly disconnects AutoBot from your account.
Do I need any technical knowledge?+
None. Our team handles the full setup on your onboarding call. No code, no configuration files, no technical background needed.
